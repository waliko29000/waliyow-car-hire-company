💰 My Store Credit

Refunds are returned as store credit in your Waliko account, not to M-Pesa. Only paid cars still waiting for pickup can be refunded.

1. Find your car under Cars available for refund and press Request refund.
2. Our team reviews it: Pending, Approved or Rejected.
3. When approved, the amount is added to your credit balance.
4. On your next booking, choose Pay with Store Credit at payment.

Good to know:
• Once a car has been picked up, it can no longer be refunded.
• Each booking can be refunded once.
• Your credit must cover the full amount of the booking you're paying for.
